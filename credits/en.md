# Data Sources & Credits

RankR uses data, images and software from the sources below. The rights to the respective content remain with the rights holders named. Trademarks and logos belong to their respective owners.

## Movies and TV — TMDB

Movie and TV information (titles, descriptions, cast, genres, trailer references) and posters are provided by [The Movie Database (TMDB)](https://www.themoviedb.org).

**This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.**

Use is subject to the [TMDB API Terms of Use](https://www.themoviedb.org/api-terms-of-use). Posters are loaded directly from TMDB.

## Recipes — TheMealDB

Recipes, ingredients and recipe images are provided by [TheMealDB](https://www.themealdb.com). The recipe content is the property of the respective authors and is provided via TheMealDB’s API.

## Books — Wikidata, Open Library, Wikipedia

- **Wikidata** ([wikidata.org](https://www.wikidata.org)): titles, authors, genres and publication years, licence [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Open Library** ([openlibrary.org](https://openlibrary.org), a project of the Internet Archive): metadata and book covers. Metadata is available under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); book covers belong to the respective publishers or rights holders and are shown for illustration.
- **Wikipedia** ([wikipedia.org](https://www.wikipedia.org)): factual basis for short descriptions. The description texts in RankR are independently worded and are not verbatim copies.

## Destinations and Restaurant Styles — Wikimedia Commons

The description texts for destinations and restaurant styles were written by the operator. Cover images come from [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org) articles and from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org). These images are available under various free licences (including CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain). Author, licence and source of each image are shown on the corresponding file page on Wikimedia Commons. On request we will name the author and licence of a specific image: support@schreiner-apps.de.

## Videos — YouTube

Trailers and clips are played via the embedded YouTube player. The [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) apply.

## Curated Sets

The curated sets (thematic collections) were compiled editorially by the operator. The titles they contain come from the sources named above.

## Fonts

- Bricolage Grotesque, Plus Jakarta Sans and Space Grotesk, each under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).

## Icons

- User interface icons: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC licence), provided via the Forui library.
- The “Apple” and “Google” marks in the sign-in buttons belong to Apple Inc. and Google LLC respectively.

## Open-Source Software

RankR is built on Flutter and other open-source libraries (including Forui, Riverpod, go_router, Supabase). We provide the licence texts of all included packages on request: support@schreiner-apps.de.
