# Data Deletion Request

This page explains how you can request the deletion of data associated with the app **RankR** (publisher: Benedikt Schreiner).

## 1. Delete your account and data yourself (recommended)

In the app open **Settings → More** and choose:

- **“Delete all ratings”**: removes your ratings and duels; your account remains.
- **“Delete account”**: deletes your account immediately and irreversibly. This deletes your profile, ratings, duels, watchlist, skipped suggestions, group memberships, push tokens and settings, your own categories and the groups you created.

Important:

- **Entries you created** remain after account deletion without any link to you; public entries stay visible to others. If you want these entries or uploaded images removed as well, write to us (see Section 3).
- **Subscriptions** are not ended by deleting the account. Please cancel an existing Premium subscription beforehand in the subscription settings of your Apple or Google account.
- **Local data** (session, settings) is removed by uninstalling the app.

## 2. Data as a guest (group link on the web)

If you took part as a guest via a group link, you do not have a regular account. You can remove locally stored access data at any time via your browser settings (clear site data); access to your guest account is then lost. To have your name and ratings deleted on our server, request deletion by email (Section 3).

## 3. Request deletion by email

Send an email to **support@schreiner-apps.de** with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and tell us:

- the email address of your account (for Apple sign-in, the address used, possibly an anonymous relay address), or, for guest participation, the name you used and the group,
- whether the entire account or only specific content (e.g. individual entries or images) should be deleted.

To protect against misuse we may need to verify your identity. You will receive a confirmation within **30 days**.

## 4. What we retain after deletion

We do not keep a copy of your profile or ratings. Backups held by the hosting provider are removed in the course of regular overwriting. Purchases and payment receipts are held by Apple or Google and are subject to their retention.

## 5. Data we do not store

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. There are therefore no usage profiles or behavioural data beyond the data named in the Privacy Policy.

More details can be found in our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/en.html).
