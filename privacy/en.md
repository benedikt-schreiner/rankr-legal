## 2. Principle

RankR is an app for rating and comparing movies, series, books, recipes, restaurants, destinations and your own entries – on your own or in groups. This requires a user account (or, for guest access to a group, an anonymous guest account, see Section 6). We only process the data needed for your account, ratings, groups, notifications and purchases. There is no advertising, no advertising profiling and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 3. User Account and Sign-in

You can sign in in three ways:

- With email address and password. The password is stored only as a hash; confirmation and password-reset emails are sent to this address.
- With “Sign in with Apple”. Apple sends us a user identifier, your email address (optionally an anonymous relay address) and possibly your name.
- With “Sign in with Google”. Google sends us a user identifier, your email address and possibly your name.

When signing in via Apple or Google, the respective provider’s privacy policy also applies. To keep you signed in, the app stores a session token on your device.

## 4. Profile and Visibility to Others

Your account has a profile with display name, username (handle), avatar colour and your chosen app language. At sign-up, display name and handle are pre-filled from your name or from the part of your email address before the “@”, and you can change them at any time.

Display name, handle and avatar colour are visible to other signed-in users and can be found via search so they can invite you to groups. Your email address is not visible to other users.

## 5. Ratings, Entries and Groups

We store the following content with your account:

- Ratings (scores 1–10), duel decisions, your watchlist and skipped suggestions
- Categories and entries you create (title, description, tags, optionally an image)
- Groups you create or belong to, group rounds, and entries or sets suggested or added in groups
- Purchases and unlocked premium features or sets (only the information about what is unlocked for your account; no payment data)
- App settings such as appearance and language, as well as your notification settings

Your individual ratings are not visible to other users. In groups, shared rankings are calculated from the members’ ratings and duels; members see the names and avatars of the other members as well as the shared results.

You choose the visibility of your own entries: private, your groups, or public. Public entries are visible to all users. Uploaded images are served via an unguessable address but are not additionally access-protected – so please do not upload images with sensitive content or identifiable people.

## 6. Guest Access via Group Link (Web)

The creator of a group can generate a time-limited invitation link. Anyone who opens this link can take part in the group as a guest without their own RankR account. The following applies:

- When joining, an anonymous guest account is created (without email address and without password). You enter a name that is visible to the members of the group. You may also choose a pseudonym.
- Before joining, we ask for your explicit consent that your name and ratings are processed for this group. We store the time of consent.
- Your ratings and duel decisions are included in the group’s shared ranking just like those of signed-in members.
- In your browser, the guest website stores an identifier of your guest account and interim state locally (Local Storage) so that you are back in the group when you revisit the link. You can delete this data at any time via your browser settings; access to that guest account is then lost.
- The static files of the guest website are hosted at and delivered from STRATO (STRATO GmbH, Berlin, Germany). For technical reasons, server log data is generated (IP address, time, page requested). A data processing agreement is in place with STRATO.
- Group owners can revoke links at any time.

Deleting your guest data: write to support@schreiner-apps.de and tell us the name you used and the group (see also our “Data Deletion” page).

## 7. Notifications (Push)

If you enable notifications in the app and the operating system grants permission, we send you push notifications, e.g. reminders, group activity or round deadline notices. For this we store:

- a device- and app-specific push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), the platform (iOS/Android) and your language,
- your choice of which notification categories you want to receive, and
- a simple delivery log (user, trigger, time) to avoid duplicate notifications.

Push messages are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and – on iOS – the Apple Push Notification Service. The push token and the message content are transmitted in the process. You can disable notifications at any time in the app (notification settings) or in your system settings. The push token is then no longer used; it is removed when you delete your account.

We use Firebase solely for push delivery. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or advertising features are not integrated.

## 8. Access to Photos

When you add an image to an entry, the app opens your device’s photo picker. Only the image you select is uploaded. The app does not access the rest of your library.

## 9. Locally Stored Data

On your device, the app stores the session token and settings such as appearance (light/dark), language and the most recently chosen Discover filter. The session token is removed when you sign out; everything else is removed when you uninstall the app.

## 10. External Services and Recipients

**Supabase** (Supabase Inc., San Francisco, USA)
Supabase provides our backend: sign-in, database, storage for uploaded images and server-side functions. All data listed in Sections 3 to 7 is stored there. For technical reasons, your IP address is processed as well. The project runs in the “West Europe (London)” region, so the data is stored on servers in the United Kingdom. A data processing agreement is in place with Supabase. More info: [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

**Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland / Google LLC, USA)
Delivery of push notifications, see Section 7. More info: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

**Apple and Google (sign-in)**
Only if you sign in via Apple or Google, see Section 3.

**Apple App Store / Google Play Store (purchases and subscriptions)**
For purchases and subscriptions, Apple or Google handle payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds under their own privacy policies. RankR receives no payment data, only the confirmation of which product is unlocked for your account, and stores this with your account.

**TMDB, TheMealDB, Open Library, Wikimedia (images and content)**
The app loads movie and series posters directly from TMDB’s servers (image.tmdb.org), recipe images from TheMealDB (themealdb.com), book covers from Open Library / the Internet Archive (covers.openlibrary.org) and images of destinations and restaurant styles from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org; Wikimedia Foundation, USA). Your IP address and standard technical information (e.g. device type) are transmitted to these providers. No other personal data is transferred. We mirror some of the images to our own storage; in that case only Supabase is contacted.

**Detail information (TMDB, TheMealDB, Open Library)**
When you open the details of an entry (e.g. cast, ingredients, synopsis), our server retrieves this information from the providers named above and caches it. Only the identifier of the entry is transmitted – no personal data about you.

**YouTube (trailers)**
In the detail view of movies and series, trailers and clips can be played via the embedded YouTube player (Google). Your IP address and technical information are transmitted to YouTube/Google in the process; YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies. Google’s privacy policy applies ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

For the sources of our content and the respective licences see the page [Data Sources & Credits](../credits/en.html).

## 11. Legal Basis

Processing is based on

- Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app, your account and purchased content under the terms of use),
- Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and reliable operation, e.g. when loading images and preventing duplicate notifications),
- Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent, in particular for guest access and push notifications; you can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future), and
- Section 25(1) TDDDG (German law), where information on your device is accessed (e.g. push token, local storage) and consent is obtained.

## 12. International Data Transfers

Our data is stored at Supabase on servers in the United Kingdom (London). The EU Commission has adopted an adequacy decision for the United Kingdom. Supabase, Google, Apple and the image and content providers may additionally process data on servers outside the EU, in particular in the USA (e.g. through access by the company itself or when loading images). Supabase Inc., Google LLC and Apple Inc. are certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework; where required, the EU Commission’s standard contractual clauses additionally apply.

## 13. Retention and Deletion

We store your data for as long as your account exists. In the app, under Settings → More, you can delete at any time:

- “Delete all ratings”: removes your ratings and duels.
- “Delete account”: permanently deletes your account, including profile, ratings, duels, watchlist, group memberships, push tokens, your own categories and the groups you created.

Entries you created remain after account deletion without any link to you; public entries stay visible to others. If you want these entries or uploaded images removed as well, contact us at support@schreiner-apps.de.

Data that Apple or Google store in connection with purchases is their responsibility; an existing subscription is not ended automatically when you delete your RankR account and must be cancelled in the respective store. Statutory retention obligations (e.g. under tax law) affect us only for data arising with us; payment receipts are kept by the stores.

Details on deletion can be found on the page [Data Deletion](../deletion/en.html).

## 14. Minimum Age

RankR is not intended for children. Use is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, please contact support@schreiner-apps.de and we will delete the account.

## 15. Your Rights (Art. 15–22 GDPR)

You have the right to:

- Access your stored data (Art. 15)
- Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
- Erasure (Art. 17)
- Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
- Data portability (Art. 20)
- Objection to processing (Art. 21)
- Withdraw consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

To exercise your rights, contact: support@schreiner-apps.de

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (ldi.nrw.de), which is responsible for us.

## 16. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or legal requirements change. The current version is available in the app and on this page.
