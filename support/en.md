# Support

Do you have a question, found a bug or need help with RankR? We are happy to help.

## Contact

Email: [support@schreiner-apps.de](mailto:support@schreiner-apps.de)

We aim to respond to requests within a few business days.

## Common topics

**Premium or a purchased set is not unlocked**
First check in the app whether “Restore purchases” unlocks it again, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple or Google account you purchased with. If the problem persists, contact us by email with the purchase date and, if available, the receipt from the App Store or Google Play.

**Cancelling a subscription or getting a refund**
Subscriptions are managed in the respective store: iPhone/iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

**Sign-in or password**
Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen. If you registered with Apple or Google, sign in with that provider again.

**Reporting content and blocking users**
Report entries with the flag icon at the top left of the detail view. Report groups and members in the group editor (“Report group” or the “…” menu next to a member); you can also block members there. Manage blocked users under Settings → Blocked users. We usually review reports within 24 hours. You can also write to us directly: support@schreiner-apps.de.

**Wrong information about movies, books, recipes or destinations**
Much of the data comes from third parties (see [Data Sources & Credits](../credits/en.html)). If you notice an error, we appreciate a note by email.

**Bugs or crashes**
Please describe as precisely as possible what you did when the problem occurred, along with your device model, OS version and app version.

**Deleting your account or data**
See [Data Deletion](../deletion/en.html).
